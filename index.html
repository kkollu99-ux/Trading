Help Center

What is KYC verification?KYC confirms your identity using a government document so we can enable deposits and withdrawals on your account.
How do deposits and withdrawals work?Start a chat from the Deposit or Withdraw tab. Our support team verifies your request and credits or debits your account balance.
What are Stop Loss and Take Profit?They automatically close an open position once your floating profit or loss reaches the dollar amount you set.
How is margin calculated?Margin = (lots × 100 units × price) ÷ multiplier. It's held from your balance while a position is open and released when you close it.
How do I contact support?Use the Online Service tab to open a live chat with our team at any time.
